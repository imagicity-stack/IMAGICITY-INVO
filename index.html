Compliance notes

GST-ready defaults and invoice anatomy

Required invoice fields

  • Unique invoice/quotation number
  • Date of issue and due date
  • Supplier GSTIN (Imagicity) and client GSTIN
  • Place of supply and whether interstate
  • HSN/SAC, quantity, rate, taxable value
  • Tax split: CGST & SGST (intrastate) or IGST (interstate)
  • Rounding and grand total with words

Typical marketing SAC codes

Use SAC 99836 (Advertising) or 99837 (Marketing services). Media buying with markup can still fall under 99836.

Note: Reverse charge is generally not applicable for standard agency billing; mention if client is responsible.

Payment preferences

Support UPI, IMPS/NEFT, and cheque. Include bank details and UPI ID within the notes field. Auto-reminders can be added via Firebase Cloud Functions.
